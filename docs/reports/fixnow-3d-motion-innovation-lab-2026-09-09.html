Section 4 · Flutter Implementation

How 3D Animations Integrate Into FixNow Without Lag

Delivering 3D animations without compromising 60 FPS / 120 FPS buttery scrolling requires strict architectural separation:

1. Lightweight 3D Geometry (glTF / GLB)

Keep 3D models under 250 KB with low polygon counts (under 5,000 vertices) and baking ambient occlusion textures. This guarantees 0ms load stutter even on low-end budget smartphones.

2. Skia / Impeller Shader Pipeline

Flutter 3.x utilizes the Impeller rendering backend on iOS and Vulkan on Android. 3D matrix transforms (Matrix4) execute directly on the GPU without triggering widget tree rebuilds.

3. Battery & Thermals Auto-Pausing

Every 3D render loop automatically suspends (TickerProvider.stop()) when the screen is navigated away from or when the user scrolls the card off-screen (Intersection Observer).
