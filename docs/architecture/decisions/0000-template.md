# ADR-0000: Decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: Team or role

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and forces that make a decision necessary.

## Decision

State the chosen approach precisely, including its scope and boundaries.

## Consequences

List positive outcomes, costs, risks, migration needs, and operational effects.

## Alternatives considered

Describe viable alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Validation

State how the decision will be tested or reviewed and what evidence could cause reconsideration.
